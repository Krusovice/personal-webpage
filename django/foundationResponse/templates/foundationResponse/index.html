Foundation Response Predictor

An ML model trained on non-linear FE models, to predict a foundation response during applied loading.

Currently only a linear regression model has been deployed.

The app is working and currently based on a dataset with fixed distributed loads of 100 kPa.

Subgrades in layers of 0.5 m intervals are utilized. Mohr-Coulomb material parameters have been applied with phi=40° and cohesion of 300 kPa (low yield, but tension cutoff applied).
